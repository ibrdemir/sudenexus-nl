About sudeNEXUS

A European centre for professional development, mobility and international learning.

Who we are

A dedicated partner for international education

sudeNEXUS is a Netherlands-based organisation specialising in professional development and international mobility programmes for schools, vocational education and training institutions, adult education organisations and their staff across Europe.

With over 11 years of experience in international education and mobility project management, sudeNEXUS has grown into a trusted partner for education organisations across the continent. We have walked this road long enough to know what works, what inspires and what creates lasting impact.

What drives us

Our foundation

01

What we dream

A Europe where every professional in education has genuine access to international learning, regardless of where they work or what their institution can afford. Borders should be opportunities, not barriers.

02

What we believe

Professional development works best when it combines rigorous learning with real-world experience. A week spent training in another country, meeting colleagues from across Europe, changes how you think about your work in lasting ways.

03

What we commit

To listen before we recommend. To design programmes around the needs of each institution and each group. To be transparent about what we offer and honest when something else would serve your team better.

Our approach

How we train

Active and Experiential

Our courses are not lectures. Every session is built around practical activities, group work, real scenarios and hands-on exploration of tools your colleagues can use the moment they return.

International by Design

Each course brings together professionals from different European countries. This international mix is intentional. The exchange of perspectives and practices between participants is itself one of the most valuable parts of every programme.

Reflective and Transferable

We build structured reflection time into every course so that participants leave not just with new knowledge but with a clear action plan for how they will apply what they have learned back in their organisation.

Our reach

A network built over more than a decade

sudeNEXUS works with a network of experienced trainers, local education partners and cultural guides across all 15 of our destinations. Our trainers are active education and training professionals with direct classroom and leadership experience.

Our destinations span Northern, Western, Central and Southern Europe as well as the Eastern Mediterranean, giving us one of the most geographically diverse course networks available.

Design Your Mobility

Cannot find exactly what you need in our catalogue? Let us build it together.

Every education organisation is different. Every mobility project has its own goals, its own priorities and its own story. That is why sudeNEXUS does not only offer ready-made programmes. We work directly with schools, VET institutions, adult education organisations and their coordinators to design fully bespoke mobility programmes from the ground up.

Any Sector

School education, VET or adult learning. We work across all three and understand the specific requirements of each.

Any Mobility Type

Staff training, job shadowing, teaching assignments, learner internships or exchanges. We design for the full range of mobility activities.

Any Destination

From our existing network of 15 cities or beyond. If you have a specific destination in mind, let us know and we will explore it with you.
