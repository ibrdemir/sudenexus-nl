About sudeNEXUS Learning

A European centre for professional development, mobility and international learning.

Who we are

A dedicated partner for international education

sudeNEXUS Learning is a Netherlands-based organisation specialising in professional development and international mobility programmes for schools, vocational education and training institutions, adult education organisations and their staff across Europe.

With over 11 years of experience in international education and mobility project management, sudeNEXUS has grown into a trusted partner for education organisations across the continent. We have walked this road long enough to know what works, what inspires and what creates lasting impact.

We are not a large corporation. We are a dedicated team of education and mobility professionals who care deeply about the quality of every programme, the experience of every participant and the difference that genuine international development makes to education organisations and the people within them.

Our purpose

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To provide education professionals, institutions and learners across Europe with mobility and professional development experiences that are practical, inspiring and impactful — not just on paper, but in classrooms and organisations across the continent.

Our Vision

A Europe where every education professional has access to high-quality international professional development, where mobility is a natural part of every professional career in education, and where organisations use it as a long-term engine for growth.

What We Stand For

Quality without compromise. Respect for every professional. Cultural depth as part of learning. And partnership — we see ourselves as long-term partners to the organisations we work with, year after year, not just project by project.

Our approach

How We Train

Active and Experiential

Our courses are not lectures. Every session is built around practical activities, group work, real scenarios and hands-on exploration of tools and methods that participants can use the moment they return to work.

International by Design

Each course brings together educators from different European countries. This international mix is intentional. The exchange of perspectives, practices and experiences between participants is itself one of the most valuable parts of every programme.

Reflective and Transferable

We build structured reflection time into every course so that participants leave not just with new knowledge but with a clear action plan for how they will apply what they have learned back in their organisation.

Our expertise

Across the Full Education Landscape

While professional development courses for school education staff form the core of our work, sudeNEXUS has over 11 years of hands-on experience across the full range of education sectors and mobility types.

School Education

School Education

Professional development courses and mobility for teachers, school leaders and support staff. Our catalogue of 32 courses covers the full range of topics relevant to school education today.

VET

Vocational Education and Training

Staff mobility, teaching and training assignments, and learner internships and exchanges for VET institutions and their partners. We support short-term and long-term placements across our European network.

Adult Education

Adult Education

Professional development and mobility for adult education staff and organisations. We help adult education providers design and deliver projects that strengthen capacity, broaden networks and bring fresh perspectives to their work.

Our network

A Network Built Across Europe

sudeNEXUS Learning works with a network of experienced trainers, local education partners and cultural guides across all 15 of our destinations. Our trainers are active education and training professionals with direct classroom and school leadership experience.

Our destinations span Northern, Western, Central and Southern Europe as well as the Eastern Mediterranean, giving us one of the most geographically diverse course networks of any European training provider.

Tailored for you

Design Your Mobility

Cannot find exactly what you need in our catalogue? Let us build it together.

Every education organisation is different. Every mobility project has its own goals, its own priorities and its own story. That is why sudeNEXUS does not only offer ready-made programmes. We work directly with schools, VET institutions, adult education organisations and their coordinators to design fully bespoke mobility programmes from the ground up.

Any Sector

School education, VET or adult learning. We work across all three and understand the specific requirements of each.

Any Mobility Type

Staff training, job shadowing, teaching assignments, learner internships or exchanges. We design for the full range of mobility activities.

Any Destination

From our existing network of 15 cities or beyond. If you have a specific destination in mind, let us know and we will explore it with you.

Whether you are planning your first mobility project or looking for a trusted long-term partner, we would love to hear from you.
