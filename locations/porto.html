Porto

One of Europe's most celebrated and characterful cities.

Why Porto?

Porto is one of Europe's most celebrated and characterful cities. Perched above the Douro River in northwest Portugal, it combines a UNESCO-listed historic centre, a world-famous wine culture and a thriving contemporary arts scene with the warmth and accessibility that makes it one of the continent's most beloved professional development destinations.

Porto offers a rich combination of European heritage and contemporary innovation. The Baixa and Ribeira districts provide a walkable, human-scale setting for a week of professional development, while the city's international connections make it easily accessible from across Europe. For education professionals, Porto represents a genuinely distinctive experience: a city that is simultaneously deeply rooted in history and vigorously forward-looking.

UNESCO World Heritage Setting

Porto's historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, providing an extraordinary backdrop for professional development. The Ribeira waterfront, the iconic Dom Luis I bridge and the azulejo-tiled facades of the Baixa create an environment that is genuinely inspiring and memorable for every participant.

A City of Culture and Innovation

Porto has undergone a remarkable cultural renaissance in recent years, establishing itself as one of Europe's most exciting cities for contemporary art, design and creative industries. For courses on innovative teaching, creativity and digital transformation, the city offers a living example of cultural reinvention.

Accessible from Across Europe

Porto's Francisco Sa Carneiro Airport is one of Portugal's busiest international airports, with direct flights from virtually every European country. The city centre is 20 minutes from the airport by metro, making Porto one of the most straightforward destinations in our network to reach.

Getting to Porto

Porto is served by Francisco Sa Carneiro Airport, with direct flights from most major European cities. The airport metro line connects directly to the city centre in around 20 minutes, making arrival and departure straightforward. Porto is also well connected by rail within Portugal, and the city centre is compact and very walkable.

Cultural Highlights

A selection of the experiences that make Porto such a memorable destination for our participants.

The Ribeira and the Douro

The UNESCO-listed Ribeira waterfront district is one of the most beautiful urban spaces in Europe. The colourful facades, the riverside cafes and the view across to Vila Nova de Gaia with its famous wine lodges create a setting that is unforgettable for participants arriving for the first time.

Port Wine Culture

Porto gave its name to port wine, and the wine lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia, visible across the Douro from the Ribeira, offer a unique cultural experience. An optional evening visit to a wine lodge is one of the most popular activities of any Porto course week.

The Livraria Lello

One of the most beautiful bookshops in the world, the Livraria Lello is a neo-Gothic masterpiece that has become an iconic symbol of Porto's literary and cultural heritage. For educators interested in the relationship between beautiful spaces and learning, it is a genuinely thought-provoking place to visit.

Contemporary Arts and Architecture

Porto has invested heavily in contemporary culture, with institutions such as the Serralves Museum of Contemporary Art and the Casa da Musica concert hall representing some of the finest examples of contemporary architecture in Europe. The city is a living case study in how heritage and innovation can coexist.

Courses Available in Porto

A wide range of our professional development courses are available in Porto, spanning digital transformation, innovative teaching, inclusion, sustainability, school leadership and more. Porto is particularly well suited to courses on creativity, cultural heritage and contemporary innovation.
