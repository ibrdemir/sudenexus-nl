Vienna

Imperial elegance meets contemporary European thinking.

Berlin All Destinations Barcelona

Why Vienna?

For centuries the seat of the Habsburg Empire and the centre of European intellectual and artistic life, Vienna today combines extraordinary imperial grandeur with a vibrant contemporary culture, a world-leading quality of life and a strong tradition of progressive thinking in education and social policy. For education professionals, Vienna is a uniquely inspiring destination. The city has a long and distinguished tradition in pedagogy and child development, and has been home to some of the most influential thinkers in educational psychology and social learning theory. Its world-class museums, its magnificent architecture and its warm, welcoming atmosphere make it one of the most complete and enjoyable destinations in our network.

Vienna has a quality that is difficult to describe but immediately felt: a sense of civilised ease, of culture taken seriously and enjoyed fully, that permeates everyday life in the city. Our participants consistently describe Vienna as one of the most pleasurable and intellectually stimulating destinations in our network.

Why Train in Vienna?

A World Capital of Culture and Ideas

Vienna has been at the centre of European intellectual and artistic life for centuries. From Beethoven and Mozart to Freud and Klimt, the city's contribution to European culture is unmatched. For education professionals, this cultural density provides an extraordinary backdrop for professional development.

Outstanding Educational Tradition

Austria has a strong and longstanding tradition in education research, pedagogy and school development. Vienna in particular has been home to influential thinkers in educational psychology and learning theory whose ideas continue to shape European education.

European Institutions and Policy

Vienna is home to numerous major international organisations and European institutions, including the United Nations Office at Vienna and the OSCE. For courses on European values, global citizenship and international cooperation, the city provides a uniquely appropriate context.

Getting to Vienna

Vienna International Airport (VIE) is one of the busiest in Central Europe and is served by direct flights from virtually every European country. The airport is connected to the city centre by the City Airport Train in approximately 16 minutes. Vienna is also exceptionally well served by high-speed rail from Munich, Budapest, Prague and beyond.

Cultural Highlights

A selection of the experiences that make Vienna such a memorable destination for our participants.

The Kunsthistorisches Museum and the Imperial Collections

The Kunsthistorisches Museum houses one of the greatest art collections in the world, including masterpieces by Raphael, Titian, Bruegel and Vermeer. Combined with the Imperial collections of the Hofburg Palace, it offers cultural riches of extraordinary depth.

Schonbrunn Palace and the Imperial Gardens

The magnificent Schonbrunn Palace and its formal gardens are among the finest examples of Baroque architecture and landscape design in Europe, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. A visit is one of the highlights of every course week in Vienna.

The Viennese Coffeehouse

The Viennese coffeehouse is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, a unique institution of European intellectual and social life. Spending an afternoon reading, talking and thinking in one of Vienna's historic coffeehouses is an experience that participants treasure.

The Vienna Philharmonic and the Music Scene

Vienna's musical heritage is unparalleled. From the Vienna Philharmonic and the Vienna State Opera to the many smaller concert halls and music venues throughout the city, the musical life of Vienna is a constant and enriching presence during every course week.
