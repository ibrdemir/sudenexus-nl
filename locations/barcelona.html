Barcelona

Where creativity and education meet the Mediterranean.

Vienna All Destinations Athens

Why Barcelona?

A city of extraordinary architectural beauty, Mediterranean warmth and relentless cultural energy, Barcelona has long been a magnet for educators, artists, innovators and creative thinkers from around the world. For education professionals, Barcelona is an outstanding destination. The city has a strong and distinctive educational tradition, with a long history of progressive pedagogy, innovative school design and community-centred learning. Its extraordinary architectural heritage, from the genius of Antoni Gaudi to the bold modernism of the Eixample district, provides a constant visual stimulus that enriches every aspect of the professional development experience.

Barcelona has a quality that is immediately energising. The combination of outstanding architecture, Mediterranean light, a vibrant street life and genuinely warm and welcoming people makes it one of the most enjoyable and stimulating destinations in our entire network. Our participants consistently leave Barcelona inspired.

Why Train in Barcelona?

A City of Creative Excellence

Barcelona is one of the world's great centres of design, architecture and creative thinking. For courses on creativity, arts education, design thinking and innovative teaching methods, there is no more appropriate or inspiring setting.

A Strong Tradition of Progressive Education

Catalonia has a distinctive and longstanding tradition of progressive education, with a history of innovative approaches to community learning, bilingual education and student-centred pedagogy that makes Barcelona a particularly stimulating destination for education professionals.

Mediterranean Culture and European Identity

Barcelona sits at the crossroads of European and Mediterranean culture, with a unique identity shaped by Catalan language and tradition, Spanish culture and a powerful international outlook. For courses on intercultural learning, European values and global citizenship, it provides a rich and distinctive context.

Getting to Barcelona

Barcelona El Prat Airport (BCN) is served by direct flights from most European countries, including numerous low-cost routes that keep travel costs well within Erasmus+ grant budgets. The airport is connected to the city centre by direct train and metro in approximately 35 minutes.

Cultural Highlights

A selection of the experiences that make Barcelona such a memorable destination for our participants.

The Sagrada Familia

Gaudi's extraordinary unfinished basilica is one of the most remarkable buildings in the world and the defining symbol of Barcelona. A guided visit is among the most memorable cultural experiences in our entire programme network.

Park Guell and the Gaudi Legacy

Park Guell, with its extraordinary mosaic terraces and organic architectural forms, offers a view over the city and an encounter with Gaudi's visionary approach to space, nature and design that is genuinely unforgettable.

The Gothic Quarter and Las Ramblas

Barcelona's Gothic Quarter, with its medieval streets, Roman ruins and hidden squares, sits just minutes from the famous Las Ramblas promenade. It is one of the most atmospheric and historically layered urban neighbourhoods in Europe.

La Boqueria and the Food Culture

Barcelona's La Boqueria market is one of the finest food markets in Europe, a sensory experience of colour, flavour and Mediterranean abundance. The city's culinary culture, from its tapas bars and seafood restaurants to its world-class fine dining, is a highlight of every course week.
