Cappadocia

One of the world's most extraordinary landscapes. An unforgettable setting for professional growth.

Naples All Destinations Istanbul

Why Cappadocia?

Located in the heart of Anatolia in central Turkey, Cappadocia is a landscape of fairy chimneys, volcanic rock formations, underground cities, cave churches and ancient Christian monasteries carved directly into the living rock over thousands of years. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most visually extraordinary environments in the world. For education professionals, Cappadocia offers something that no other destination in our network can provide: the experience of learning and reflecting in a setting of such exceptional natural and historical beauty that it fundamentally changes how participants think about the relationship between environment, culture and learning. Courses in Cappadocia consistently produce among our highest participant satisfaction ratings, and the destination is particularly well suited to courses on cultural heritage, sustainability, outdoor and experiential learning, and wellbeing.

Cappadocia is a destination that has to be seen to be believed. Participants who come here describe the experience as genuinely life-changing, not just professionally enriching but personally transformative. The combination of extraordinary landscape, deep history, warm hospitality and the unique experience of staying in a cave hotel makes Cappadocia one of the most memorable weeks of any education professional's career.

Why Train in Cappadocia?

An Extraordinary Natural Landscape

The volcanic landscape of Cappadocia, with its iconic fairy chimneys, rose valleys and dramatic rock formations, is one of the most visually spectacular environments on earth. For courses on environmental education, outdoor learning and sustainability, it provides an incomparable setting.

Extraordinary Cultural and Historical Heritage

Cappadocia was one of the most important centres of early Christianity, and its landscape is filled with rock-cut churches, underground cities and monastic communities dating back to the first centuries of the Christian era. For courses on cultural heritage, European history and religious diversity, it is an unparalleled destination.

Wellbeing and Reflective Learning

The peace, the beauty and the extraordinary atmosphere of Cappadocia create ideal conditions for reflective professional development. Courses on teacher wellbeing, mindfulness, social-emotional learning and burnout prevention are particularly well suited to this destination.

Getting to Cappadocia

Cappadocia is served by two regional airports: Kayseri Airport (ASR) and Nevsehir Kapadokya Airport (NAV). Both airports receive direct flights from Istanbul and other major Turkish cities, with connecting flights available from most European airports. Transfer from Kayseri to the main tourist areas of Urgup, Goreme and Nevsehir takes approximately 75 minutes by shuttle.

Cultural Highlights

A selection of the experiences that make Cappadocia such a memorable destination for our participants.

Hot Air Balloon Flight over the Fairy Chimneys

A hot air balloon flight at dawn over the rock formations of Cappadocia is one of the most memorable experiences available anywhere in our network. Watching the sun rise over the extraordinary landscape from above is an experience that participants consistently describe as unforgettable.

The Underground Cities of Derinkuyu and Kaymakli

Cappadocia is home to some of the most extraordinary underground cities ever created by human beings, carved to depths of up to 85 metres and capable of housing thousands of people. A guided visit is among the most remarkable cultural and historical experiences in our programme.

The Open Air Museum of Goreme

The Goreme Open Air Museum is a UNESCO World Heritage Site containing some of the finest examples of Byzantine rock-cut architecture and fresco painting in existence. It is among the most important cultural heritage sites in Turkey and in our entire destination network.

The Rose Valley and the Walking Trails

Cappadocia's extraordinary walking trails through the Rose Valley, the Love Valley and the surrounding landscape offer an experience of natural beauty and historical discovery that is unique among our destinations. Guided walks are a regular feature of our cultural programme here.
