Skien

Scandinavian education tradition in a setting of extraordinary natural beauty.

Why Skien?

Skien is the birthplace of Henrik Ibsen and the regional capital of Telemark in southern Norway. Surrounded by lakes, forests and the dramatic Norwegian landscape, it offers a distinctive Scandinavian professional development experience with strong connections to Nordic education traditions that have shaped progressive pedagogy across Europe.

Skien offers something genuinely different from our other destinations: the chance to experience Scandinavian educational culture firsthand, in a setting of extraordinary natural beauty. The city's connections to progressive Nordic pedagogy make it particularly relevant for courses on innovative teaching methods, inclusion and wellbeing. For education professionals who want to understand how some of the world's most effective education systems actually work, Skien provides direct access to that tradition.

The Nordic Education Tradition

Norway consistently ranks among the world's top-performing education systems. Skien sits at the heart of this tradition, offering participants direct exposure to the values, structures and pedagogical approaches that have made Scandinavian education a global reference point. For any school development team working on curriculum innovation, wellbeing or inclusion, this context is genuinely invaluable.

Extraordinary Natural Setting

Skien is surrounded by one of the most beautiful natural landscapes in Europe. The lakes, forests and waterways of the Telemark region provide a setting for outdoor learning activities, wellbeing sessions and cultural excursions that no urban destination can match. For courses on sustainability, outdoor learning and teacher wellbeing, this environment is a genuine asset.

The Birthplace of Ibsen

Skien is the birthplace of Henrik Ibsen, one of the most significant playwrights in world literature. The city celebrates this heritage with museums, walking trails and cultural events that connect participants to a rich tradition of humanistic inquiry and storytelling. For educators interested in drama, literature and the arts in education, Skien offers a distinctive and inspiring context.

Getting to Skien

Skien is served by Notodden Airport and is also accessible via Oslo Airport Gardermoen, with coach and rail connections to the city. Oslo is approximately two and a half hours from Skien by road or rail, making it straightforward to reach from international connections. We provide full travel guidance for all participants to ensure arrival and departure are smooth.

Cultural Highlights

A selection of the experiences that make Skien such a memorable destination for our participants.

The Ibsen Museum

The Henrik Ibsen Museum in Skien tells the story of the playwright's early life in the city and his journey to becoming one of the most influential writers in history. For educators interested in drama, creative writing and humanities education, it is a genuinely moving and thought-provoking place to visit.

The Telemark Canal and Waterways

The Telemark Canal is one of Norway's most celebrated engineering achievements and one of its most beautiful natural corridors. A boat trip along the canal through locks, forests and open lakes is one of the most distinctive cultural experiences in our entire destination network.

Nordic Nature and Outdoor Life

The forests, lakes and mountains surrounding Skien offer exceptional opportunities for outdoor activities that connect directly to course themes around wellbeing, sustainability and outdoor learning. Swimming in the lakes in summer, forest walks in autumn and the extraordinary Nordic light at any time of year create an experience that participants remember long after they return home.

Telemark Culture and Tradition

The Telemark region has a rich cultural heritage, from the traditional folk music and crafts of the area to the local food culture centred on Norwegian seafood, game and artisan produce. Social evenings during course weeks in Skien offer a warm and genuine introduction to Norwegian hospitality.

Courses Available in Skien

A range of our professional development courses are available in Skien, with a particular focus on innovative teaching methods, inclusion and wellbeing, sustainability and outdoor learning. Skien is an especially strong choice for schools working on student wellbeing, nature-based learning or engagement with Nordic pedagogical approaches.
