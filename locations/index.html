Our Locations

15 destinations across Europe and the Mediterranean. Choose the city that inspires you.

Where Will You Learn Next?

At Sude Nexus we believe that where you learn is as important as what you learn. That is why we have built a network of 15 carefully chosen destinations across Europe and the Mediterranean, each offering a unique combination of professional training quality, cultural richness and genuine inspiration.

From the canal-lined streets of Amsterdam and Bruges to the sun-drenched Mediterranean shores of Barcelona, Athens and Antalya, from the dynamic energy of Berlin and Hamburg to the ancient crossroads of Istanbul, from the Douro riverfront of Porto to the Nordic forests of Skien and the classical heritage of Naples, every Sude Nexus destination adds something distinctive to the professional development experience.

All 15 locations offer fully equipped professional training facilities, a rich cultural and social programme, and the full support of our local coordination team. Your Erasmus+ grant covers your course fee, travel, accommodation and subsistence. All you need to do is choose your destination.

More Than a Backdrop

Every destination in our network has been chosen because it actively enhances the learning experience.

Carefully Chosen

Every city in our network has been selected because it adds genuine value to the professional development experience. Rich history, cultural diversity, natural beauty, architectural significance and educational tradition are among the qualities we look for in every destination.

Fully Supported

In every location we work with experienced local coordinators, trusted training facilities and knowledgeable cultural guides. Our participants never feel alone in an unfamiliar city. From arrival to departure, our local teams are on hand to make every experience smooth and memorable.

Erasmus+ Budget Friendly

All 15 destinations are accessible by direct flights from most European countries and offer accommodation options at a range of price points. We provide detailed budget guidance for every location so that coordinators can plan their Erasmus+ grant allocation with confidence.

Let Us Help You Decide

Choosing the right destination for your Erasmus+ mobility depends on many factors: your course topic, your grant budget, your travel connections and of course the personal preferences of your participants. Our team is happy to advise on which destination works best for your specific situation.
