Our Locations

13 destinations across Europe and the Mediterranean. Choose the city that inspires you.

Where Will You Learn Next?

At Sude Nexus we believe that where you learn is as important as what you learn. That is why we have built a network of 13 carefully chosen destinations across Europe and the Mediterranean, each offering a unique combination of professional training quality, cultural richness and genuine inspiration.

From the canal-lined streets of Amsterdam and Bruges to the sun-drenched Mediterranean shores of Barcelona, Athens and Antalya, from the dynamic energy of Berlin and Hamburg to the ancient crossroads of Istanbul and the classical heritage of Naples and Athens, every Sude Nexus destination adds something distinctive to the professional development experience.

All 13 locations offer fully equipped professional training facilities, a rich cultural and social programme, and the full support of our local coordination team. Your Erasmus+ grant covers your course fee, travel, accommodation and subsistence. All you need to do is choose your destination.

More Than a Backdrop

Carefully Chosen

Every city in our network has been selected because it adds genuine value to the professional development experience. Rich history, cultural diversity, natural beauty, architectural significance and educational tradition are among the qualities we look for in every destination.

Fully Supported

In every location we work with experienced local coordinators, trusted training facilities and knowledgeable cultural guides. Our participants never feel alone in an unfamiliar city. From arrival to departure, our local teams are on hand to make every experience smooth and memorable.

Erasmus+ Budget Friendly

All 13 destinations are accessible by direct flights from most European countries and offer accommodation options at a range of price points. We provide detailed budget guidance for every location so that coordinators can plan their Erasmus+ grant allocation with confidence.
