Our Services

Everything your organisation needs for a successful international mobility experience, in one place.

Who we are

More Than a Course Provider

sudeNEXUS Learning is not simply a catalogue of courses. We are a full-service professional development and mobility partner. From the moment your organisation begins planning a mobility project to the day your participants return home with new skills and fresh perspectives, we are with you at every step.

Our services are designed to remove the complexity from international mobility and replace it with clarity, confidence and genuine support. Whether you are coordinating a group of teachers on a professional development week, arranging a job shadowing placement for a colleague, or managing a learner exchange programme for VET students, we handle the details so you can focus on the impact.

What we provide

Our Services

Professional Development Courses

Our catalogue of 32 internationally recognised courses covers the full range of topics relevant to school education, VET and adult learning today. All courses are one week in duration, delivered in English, and designed for international mixed groups of education professionals. Every course includes full documentation for grant reporting.

Bespoke Programme Design

Cannot find exactly what you need in our catalogue? We design fully customised training programmes, mobility projects and learning experiences from scratch. Tell us your goals and your context and we will build something that fits perfectly.

Job Shadowing Placements

We arrange job shadowing placements for education professionals who want to spend time in a partner institution abroad. Whether you are looking for a host institution in one of our 15 destinations or need help matching a colleague with the right placement, our team manages the entire process.

Learner Mobility and Internships

We support VET institutions and adult education organisations in arranging learner internships and exchange programmes across Europe. We help with partner matching, placement coordination, participant preparation and all administrative requirements for funded learner mobility.

Project Consultancy

Not sure where to start with your mobility project application? We advise schools, VET institutions and adult education organisations on how to structure their projects, which activities to include, how to align mobility with institutional development plans and how to meet the requirements of national agencies.

Accommodation and Logistics Support

We provide guidance and recommendations for accommodation in all 15 of our destinations, with options at different price points to suit different grant budgets. We share local transport information, city guides and practical arrival details so that every participant feels prepared and welcome.

Cultural and Social Programme

Learning does not stop at the end of the training day. We organise optional cultural visits, guided excursions and social activities in each destination so that participants experience the city they are visiting and connect with colleagues from other countries in an informal setting.

Full Documentation Package

Every participant receives a complete documentation package for grant reporting. This includes a detailed course or programme description, a letter of confirmation of participation, a certificate of attendance, a record of individual learning outcomes and a participant evaluation form.

Who we serve

Who We Work With

Schools and School Leaders

We work with primary schools, secondary schools and school leaders across Europe who want to develop their staff, strengthen their institutional profile and build international partnerships.

VET Institutions

We support vocational education and training providers in arranging both staff and learner mobility. From teaching and training assignments to learner internships in European companies and institutions.

Adult Education Organisations

We work with adult education centres, community learning providers and non-formal education organisations who want to develop their staff capacity, build European networks and bring new approaches to their learners.
