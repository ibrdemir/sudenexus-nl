Planning Your Erasmus+ Mobility?

Erasmus+ KA1 projects typically require mobility activities to take place within the project period defined in your grant agreement. When planning which course dates to book, make sure the session falls within your project period and that participants have enough lead time for preparation, travel arrangements and pre-departure activities.

If you are not yet sure of your project dates or are still in the application process, our team is happy to advise on how to factor course bookings into your planning timeline.
