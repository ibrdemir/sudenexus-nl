Entrepreneurship and Innovation in VET

Develop the entrepreneurial mindset. In your learners and in your institution.

Digital TransformationInclusion and DiversityErasmus+ KA1 VET

About This Course

The EU expects Centres of Vocational Excellence to develop an entrepreneurial culture within VET organisations including leaders, staff, teachers, trainers and learners. Entrepreneurship education is now a professional responsibility for VET trainers across every sector.

The EU EntreComp framework maps the fifteen entrepreneurship competences that VET institutions are expected to develop. The Union of Skills initiative places entrepreneurial thinking at the centre of Europe's skills agenda for the coming decade.

This course gives VET professionals a thorough, inspiring and practically grounded introduction to entrepreneurship and innovation education in VET, aligned with EntreComp, the Herning Declaration and the EU Centres of Vocational Excellence framework.

Who Should Attend

  • VET teachers and trainers wanting to develop entrepreneurial thinking in learners
  • VET institution managers developing entrepreneurship strategies
  • Erasmus+ coordinators with KA2 innovation partnerships
  • VET curriculum developers integrating EntreComp
  • In-company trainers interested in innovation approaches

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course participants will be able to:

  • Explain the EntreComp framework and apply it to VET curriculum design and learner assessment.

    You will use EntreComp as a curriculum planning tool to identify which of the fifteen competences your current programme develops and which it needs to strengthen.

    EntreCompDigital Transformation Priority

  • Design challenge-based learning experiences that develop entrepreneurial competences in vocational contexts.

    Entrepreneurial competences need to be practiced through real challenges. You will design a challenge-based learning experience that develops multiple EntreComp competences simultaneously within a vocational context.

    EntreCompLifeComp

  • Connect your institution's entrepreneurship work to the EU Centres of Vocational Excellence framework.

    Centres of Vocational Excellence require entrepreneurship and innovation to be central to institutional culture. You will understand what CoVE status requires and how your institution can work towards it.

    EntreCompHerning Declaration 2026 to 2030

  • Use design thinking and innovation methodologies with VET learners across vocational sectors.

    Design thinking is a practical problem-solving approach applicable to every vocational sector. You will have experience of using it yourself and a process for introducing it with learners.

    EntreCompDigComp 3.0 Area 5

A 7-Day Professional Development Experience

Five days of intensive training with a structured arrival day and cultural excursion. We tailor every programme to your organisation's needs.

Day 1
Sunday - Arrival and Welcome

Welcome dinner and orientation walk.

Day 2
Monday - EntreComp and the VET Innovation Agenda
MorningThe EntreComp framework and why entrepreneurship matters for every VET sector.
AfternoonParticipants experience entrepreneurial learning firsthand: a structured challenge using creativity, collaboration and initiative.
Day 3
Tuesday - Ideas and Opportunities in VET
MorningCreativity and opportunity-spotting in vocational contexts. Idea generation techniques for VET sectors.
AfternoonDesign thinking in VET: how the design process develops entrepreneurial competences naturally.
Day 4
Wednesday - Resources, Resilience and Action
MorningSelf-awareness, motivation and mobilising others. Developing the personal qualities underpinning entrepreneurial action.
AfternoonFrom idea to action: planning, initiative-taking and learning from failure in a VET context.
Day 5
Thursday - Designing Your Entrepreneurship Programme
MorningParticipants design a challenge-based entrepreneurship unit for their vocational context. Peer feedback.
AfternoonConnecting to Centres of Vocational Excellence, KA2 and institutional strategy.
Day 6
Friday - Action Planning
MorningEntrepreneurship integration plan for your VET programme.
AfternoonPresentations, certificates and farewell dinner.
Day 7
Saturday - Cultural Excursion and Departure

Guided cultural excursion. Departure at convenience.

This outline is a starting point. Contact us to discuss how we can tailor this programme for your organisation.
