VET Mobility Programmes

Professional development, staff mobility and learner exchange programmes for vocational education and training institutions across Europe.

Vocational Mobility That Goes Beyond the Placement

Erasmus+ VET mobility is one of the most powerful and underused tools available to vocational education and training institutions across Europe. A well-designed VET mobility experience does not only give learners real-world work experience in another country. It develops professional confidence, intercultural competence, language skills and the kind of adaptability that employers across every sector are increasingly demanding.

At Sude Nexus we bring over 11 years of experience in European mobility to the VET sector. We work with VET institutions across Europe to design and deliver learner placements, staff training programmes and professional development experiences that are grounded in the EU VET policy framework, aligned with the twin transition priorities of digital transformation and green skills, and delivered across a network of 13 destinations with genuine sector depth.

Our VET Mobility Programmes Services

VET Learner Placements

Short-term mobility from 10 to 89 days and long-term ErasmusPro placements from 90 to 365 days. Sector-matched, quality-assured and fully documented.

VET Staff Training Courses

8 one-week professional development courses designed specifically for VET teachers, trainers and managers. Built around the Herning Declaration 2026 to 2030 and the EU twin transition priorities.

Job Shadowing for VET Staff

Job shadowing placements for VET professionals wanting to observe and learn from partner institutions and companies in another European country.

Design Your VET Mobility

If our standard offer does not precisely fit your needs, we design bespoke VET mobility programmes, sector-specific placements and tailor-made staff training from the ground up.

Staff Training Courses

All courses are fully eligible for Erasmus+ KA1 funding and follow the 7-day Sunday to Saturday format.

EU Policy Framework

Digital Transformation

Every VET programme and placement we design addresses the digital transformation of vocational sectors, aligned with DigComp 3.0, DigCompEdu and the Herning Declaration.

Green Skills

We design VET mobility with the twin green transition at the centre, supporting institutions in integrating GreenComp sustainability competences into their vocational training.

Inclusion and Diversity

We design VET mobility with inclusion at the centre, supporting institutions in organising meaningful mobility for learners with fewer opportunities.

Quality and Erasmus Standards

All Sude Nexus VET placements are designed in accordance with the Erasmus Quality Standards for VET mobility from participant preparation through to recognition.
