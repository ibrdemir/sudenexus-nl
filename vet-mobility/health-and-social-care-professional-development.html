Health and Social Care Professional Development

The most human profession deserves the best training.

Inclusion and DiversityDigital TransformationErasmus+ KA1 VET

About This Course

Health and social care is the largest and fastest-growing employment sector in Europe. Demographic change, the ageing population, the mental health crisis and increasing complexity of care needs are creating unprecedented demand for highly trained health and social care workers.

VET plays a central role developing this workforce, training nursing assistants, care workers, social care professionals, early childhood care workers and mental health support workers. The quality of this training directly affects the quality of care that millions of Europeans receive.

This course gives VET teachers and trainers in health and social care a thorough, research-grounded engagement with person-centred care, digital health tools, inclusion in care contexts and reflective professional practice.

Who Should Attend

  • VET teachers and trainers in nursing, care, social care and allied health
  • In-company trainers and supervisors in health and social care
  • VET curriculum developers updating health and care qualifications
  • VET institution managers developing health and care programmes
  • Erasmus+ coordinators in health and social care VET institutions

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course participants will be able to:

  • Apply person-centred care principles to VET training design across health and social care contexts.

    Person-centred care is the foundation of high-quality practice. You will design VET programmes that model the very values they are trying to teach, placing the person being cared for at the absolute centre.

    Inclusion and DiversityLifeComp Social Area

  • Integrate digital health tools and technologies into vocational training.

    Electronic care records, telehealth platforms, digital medication management and assistive technologies are now standard in professional care. You will design training activities that develop genuine digital health competences.

    DigComp 3.0Digital Transformation

  • Address mental health, emotional resilience and wellbeing in health and social care VET.

    Health and social care workers have among the highest rates of burnout and compassion fatigue. VET training that addresses emotional resilience is both a professional responsibility and an ethical obligation.

    LifeComp Personal AreaInclusion and Diversity

  • Design reflective practice frameworks appropriate for health and social care VET learners.

    Reflective practice is a core professional competence in all care roles. You will have practical frameworks including portfolio approaches, guided reflection tools and peer supervision models adapted for vocational contexts.

    DigCompEdu Area 4Herning Declaration 2026 to 2030

A 7-Day Professional Development Experience

Five days of intensive training with a structured arrival day and cultural excursion. We tailor every programme to your organisation's needs.

Day 1
Sunday - Arrival and Welcome

Welcome dinner and orientation walk.

Day 2
Monday - Person-Centred Care and Values in VET
MorningPerson-centred care in EU policy. Developing values-based practice in VET learners.
AfternoonCase studies and simulation: using realistic scenarios to develop values-based practice.
Day 3
Tuesday - Digital Health in VET
MorningDigital health technologies and their implications for VET training.
AfternoonParticipants design a digital health competence activity for their own VET programme.
Day 4
Wednesday - Inclusion, Diversity and Cultural Competence
MorningCultural competence in health and social care: developing intercultural awareness in VET learners.
AfternoonDisability, neurodiversity and mental health in care contexts: inclusive training approaches.
Day 5
Thursday - Wellbeing, Resilience and Reflective Practice
MorningEmotional resilience and wellbeing in health and social care VET.
AfternoonReflective practice frameworks for VET contexts. Portfolio development and peer supervision.
Day 6
Friday - Action Planning
MorningCurriculum improvement plans for health and social care VET programmes.
AfternoonPresentations, certificates and farewell dinner.
Day 7
Saturday - Cultural Excursion and Departure

Guided cultural excursion. Departure at convenience.

This outline is a starting point. Contact us to discuss how we can tailor this programme for your organisation.
