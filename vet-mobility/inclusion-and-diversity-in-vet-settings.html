Inclusion and Diversity in VET Settings

VET is for everyone. Make sure your training reflects that.

Inclusion and DiversityErasmus+ KA1 VET15 Destinations

About This Course

VET has a unique role in European inclusion. It often reaches people who have struggled in or left mainstream education: early school leavers, learners with additional needs, those from disadvantaged backgrounds, migrants and refugees, older adults returning to work and people with disabilities.

This course gives VET teachers, trainers and managers a thorough, practical and reflective engagement with inclusion and diversity in VET settings, aligned with the Herning Declaration's emphasis on inclusion, the EU Disability Rights Strategy and the European Pillar of Social Rights.

Participants develop practical strategies for removing barriers to participation and success, and design inclusive training approaches appropriate for their own vocational sector.

Who Should Attend

  • VET teachers and trainers working with diverse learner groups
  • VET coordinators responsible for inclusion and support services
  • VET institution managers developing inclusion strategies
  • In-company trainers working with apprentices with additional needs
  • Erasmus+ coordinators managing mobility for learners with fewer opportunities

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course participants will be able to:

  • Explain the EU inclusion and diversity framework for VET and connect it to institutional practice.

    The Herning Declaration, EU Disability Rights Strategy and European Pillar of Social Rights create specific obligations for VET institutions on inclusion. You will know what these obligations mean in practice and how to evidence them in Erasmus+ reporting.

    Inclusion and DiversityHerning Declaration 2026 to 2030

  • Apply Universal Design for Learning principles to VET curriculum and training delivery.

    UDL is the most comprehensive evidence-based framework for designing accessible, flexible training. Applied in VET, it means designing programmes that work for the full range of learners from the start.

    Inclusion and DiversityLifeComp

  • Design inclusive VET mobility experiences for learners with fewer opportunities.

    Erasmus+ specifically requires VET institutions to include learners with fewer opportunities. You will have frameworks for preparing these learners for mobility, selecting appropriate placements and supporting them throughout.

    Inclusion and DiversityErasmus Quality Standards for VET

  • Build an inclusive learning environment and culture in your VET institution.

    You will develop a practical audit tool for assessing your institution's inclusion culture and a specific improvement plan that addresses culture, language, representation and expectations.

    Inclusion and DiversityLifeComp Social Area

A 7-Day Professional Development Experience

Five days of intensive training with a structured arrival day and cultural excursion. We tailor every programme to your organisation's needs.

Day 1
Sunday - Arrival and Welcome

Welcome dinner and orientation walk.

Day 2
Monday - Inclusion in the EU VET Framework
MorningThe policy framework: Herning Declaration, EU Disability Rights Strategy and European Pillar of Social Rights.
AfternoonParticipants map inclusion challenges in their own context. Case studies and peer discussion.
Day 3
Tuesday - Universal Design for Learning in VET
MorningIntroduction to UDL in vocational training contexts: Multiple Means of Representation, Expression and Engagement.
AfternoonApplying UDL to a VET training unit. Participants redesign an existing activity using UDL principles.
Day 4
Wednesday - Inclusive VET Mobility
MorningErasmus+ mobility for learners with fewer opportunities: policy, preparation and practical management.
AfternoonDesigning support programmes for VET learners who face additional barriers to mobility.
Day 5
Thursday - Building Inclusive Institutional Culture
MorningThe inclusion audit: assessing and improving the culture and practices of your VET institution.
AfternoonParticipants develop an inclusion improvement plan. Peer feedback.
Day 6
Friday - Action Planning
MorningConnecting inclusion work to Erasmus+ applications and quality documentation.
AfternoonPresentations, certificates and farewell dinner.
Day 7
Saturday - Cultural Excursion and Departure

Guided cultural excursion. Departure at convenience.

This outline is a starting point. Contact us to discuss how we can tailor this programme for your organisation.
