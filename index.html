Who we are

Your European partner for professional development

sudeNEXUS Learning is a Netherlands-based training organisation dedicated to the professional development of teachers, school leaders and education staff across Europe.

We design and deliver internationally recognised programmes that combine high-quality training with genuine cultural immersion — because the best learning happens when you are also experiencing something new.

From artificial intelligence in the classroom to teacher wellbeing, from inclusive education to outdoor learning — every programme is practical, internationally oriented and built for today's educators.

Your mobility grant covers it

All sudeNEXUS Learning courses are designed to fully meet the requirements of international mobility programmes for school education staff — including Erasmus+ Key Action 1. Our programmes include a clear transnational component, structured active learning, and professional skills development directly relevant to teaching and school leadership.

Every course comes with the documentation your organisation needs: a detailed course description, a letter of confirmation, a participant certificate and an attendance record for your reporting requirements.

Not sure where to start? Our team is happy to guide you through the process.
