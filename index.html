Your European Partner for Teacher Training

Sude Nexus is a Netherlands-based training organisation dedicated to the professional development of teachers, school leaders and education staff across Europe. We design and deliver Erasmus+ KA1 eligible courses that combine high-quality training with genuine cultural immersion.

Our courses are built around what European educators need most right now. From artificial intelligence in the classroom to teacher wellbeing, from inclusive education to outdoor learning, every programme we offer is practical, internationally oriented and ready to strengthen your school's Erasmus+ project.

We believe that the best professional development happens when learning and discovery go hand in hand. That is why every Sude Nexus course takes place in a city worth exploring.

What Makes Sude Nexus Different

At the Heart of Europe

We are based in the Netherlands, one of Europe's most internationally connected countries. Our roots in the EU mean we understand Erasmus+ from the inside, and we design every course with European quality standards at the core.

15 Destinations to Choose From

From Amsterdam and Bruges to Barcelona, Athens, Vienna, Istanbul, Porto and Skien, our locations span the full breadth of European and Mediterranean culture. Participants gain professional skills and unforgettable experiences in the same week.

A Catalogue Built for 2026 and Beyond

Our 32 courses cover both the established pillars of teacher training and the topics shaping education right now: AI for teachers, digital wellbeing, neurodiversity, mindfulness, teacher burnout prevention and brain-based learning.

Full Support from Start to Finish

From the moment your school selects a course to the day your colleagues return home, our team is with you. We handle logistics, provide all documentation for your Erasmus+ project, and make sure every participant has what they need to succeed.

100% Erasmus+ KA1 Eligible

All Sude Nexus courses are designed to fully meet the requirements of Erasmus+ Key Action 1 mobility projects for school education staff. Our programmes include a clear transnational component, structured active learning, and professional skills development directly relevant to teaching and school leadership.

Every course comes with the documentation your school needs: a detailed course description, a letter of confirmation, a participant certificate and an attendance record for your National Agency reporting.

Not sure where to start with your Erasmus+ application? Our team is happy to guide you through the process.
