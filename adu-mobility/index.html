Adult Education Mobility Programmes

Professional development, staff mobility and learner exchange programmes for adult education organisations across Europe.

Adult Education Is at the Heart of European Society

Adult education is the most diverse, most inclusive and most community-rooted sector in European education. It reaches the people that other education systems often miss: adults returning to learning after years away, migrants and refugees building new lives, older adults staying active and connected, low-skilled workers navigating a rapidly changing labour market, and community members seeking the knowledge and confidence to participate more fully in democratic life.

At Sude Nexus we have over 11 years of experience supporting adult education organisations through Erasmus+ KA1 mobility. We understand the specific character of adult learning, the EU policy framework that governs it and the practical challenges that adult education organisations face when planning and delivering international mobility.

What We Offer for Adult Education

ADU Staff Training Courses

8 professional development courses designed specifically for adult educators, facilitators, social workers, community learning professionals and adult education managers. Fully eligible for Erasmus+ KA1 ADU funding.

Job Shadowing for ADU Staff

Job shadowing placements for adult education professionals wanting to observe, learn from and exchange good practice with a partner organisation in another European country.

ADU Learner Mobility

Adult learners can participate in Erasmus+ mobility too. We support adult education organisations in planning study visits, cultural exchange and intercultural learning activities for adult learner groups.

Design Your ADU Mobility

Every adult education organisation has a unique mission. If our standard offer does not precisely fit your needs, we design bespoke ADU mobility programmes from the ground up.

EU Policy Framework

Governance and Quality

We design every ADU mobility in line with European quality standards, supporting organisations in meeting their Erasmus+ quality commitments from preparation through to impact evaluation.

Accessibility and Flexibility

We design mobility programmes that are genuinely accessible and adapted to the realities of adult learning organisations across Europe.

Digital Transformation

Every ADU course addresses the digital transformation of adult education, from AI tools for facilitators to digital skills development for adult learners.

Inclusion is at the centre of everything we design for adult education, supporting organisations working with migrants, low-skilled adults, older learners and other groups.
