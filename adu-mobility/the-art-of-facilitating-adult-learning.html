The Art of Facilitating Adult Learning

Adults learn differently. Facilitate with skill, warmth and confidence.

Inclusion and DiversityParticipation in Democratic LifeErasmus+ KA1 ADU

About This Course

Facilitating learning for adults is fundamentally different from teaching children. Adults bring decades of life experience, established identities, complex motivations and sometimes difficult histories with formal education into every learning space. They need to feel respected, relevant and in control of their own learning.

This course gives adult education professionals a thorough, evidence-based and personally transformative grounding in adult learning facilitation. Participants explore the theory and research on how adults learn, develop a rich personal facilitation toolkit and practice core skills: active listening, questioning, group management, motivating resistant learners and creating psychological safety.

The course is aligned with the European Agenda for Adult Learning 2021 to 2030, the Union of Skills 2025 and the Erasmus+ priorities of Inclusion and Diversity and Participation in Democratic Life.

Who Should Attend

  • Adult educators and facilitators at all experience levels
  • Community learning professionals and social workers
  • Literacy and basic skills tutors
  • Migration and integration workers who facilitate learning
  • Anyone who works with adult groups in formal or non-formal educational settings

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course participants will be able to:

  • Apply the key theories of adult learning to the design and facilitation of adult learning programmes.

    Andragogy, transformative learning, self-directed learning and communities of practice are the foundational theories of adult education. You will use each as a practical design tool and explain their implications clearly to colleagues and managers.

    LifeCompInclusion and Diversity

  • Facilitate adult learning groups with skill, confidence and genuine presence.

    Facilitation presence is the most important quality an adult educator can develop: the ability to be fully attentive, responsive and authentic in the group, to read the room and to create conditions where learning can happen even in difficult moments.

    LifeComp Social AreaEntreComp - Resources

  • Design learning experiences for adults that respect their experience, agency and motivations.

    The most powerful adult learning experiences start from the learner's experience, not the educator's content. You will design sessions that draw on participants' knowledge, connect to their real-life purposes and give them genuine agency.

    LifeComp Personal AreaInclusion and Diversity

  • Manage challenging group dynamics including conflict, resistance, disengagement and dominant voices.

    Every adult learning group contains people who resist, disengage, dominate or carry old wounds from previous education. You will have specific strategies for each dynamic that are honest, respectful and practically effective.

    LifeComp Social AreaEuropean Agenda for Adult Learning 2021 to 2030

  • Develop a personal facilitation philosophy grounded in adult learning principles and EU policy.

    You will leave with a written facilitation philosophy that is genuinely yours: grounded in theory, shaped by practice and connected to the EU adult learning policy agenda.

    LifeCompEuropean Agenda for Adult Learning 2021 to 2030

A 7-Day Professional Development Experience

Five days of intensive training with a structured arrival day and cultural excursion. We tailor every programme to your organisation's needs.

Day 1
Sunday - Arrival and Welcome

Welcome dinner and orientation walk.

Day 2
Monday - How Adults Learn
MorningThe foundations of adult learning: andragogy, transformative learning and self-directed learning. What the research tells us.
AfternoonParticipants reflect on their own experience as adult learners and identify conditions that supported and hindered their learning.
Day 3
Tuesday - The Art of Facilitation
MorningPresence, listening and questioning in adult facilitation. Core facilitation skills. Participants practice in pairs and triads.
AfternoonDesigning for engagement: how to open a session, sustain energy and close with meaning.
Day 4
Wednesday - Working with Groups
MorningGroup dynamics in adult learning: stages of group development, roles and invisible forces.
AfternoonManaging challenging dynamics: resistance, conflict, disengagement and dominant voices.
Day 5
Thursday - Inclusive Facilitation
MorningFacilitation for diversity: creating spaces where adults from very different backgrounds all feel welcome and able to learn.
AfternoonParticipants design and facilitate a short learning session for the group. Structured feedback.
Day 6
Friday - Reflective Practice and Personal Philosophy
MorningReflective practice as a professional habit: tools and approaches for ongoing development.
AfternoonParticipants refine their personal facilitation philosophy and action plan. Certificates and farewell dinner.
Day 7
Saturday - Cultural Excursion and Departure

Guided cultural excursion. Departure at convenience.

This outline is a starting point. Contact us to discuss how we can tailor this programme for your organisation.
