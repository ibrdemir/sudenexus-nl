Language and Communication

4 courses for education professionals across Europe.

Why This Category Matters

Language is the medium through which almost all learning happens. The ability to communicate clearly, critically evaluate what we read and hear, express ourselves creatively and engage with others across linguistic and cultural difference is not just a professional skill. It is a civic competence and a human right. The four courses in this category address language and communication in education from four distinct and complementary angles: CLIL methodology for content and language integration, creative drama as a tool for language learning and personal development, English language for professional purposes, and media literacy and critical thinking in an age of information overload.
