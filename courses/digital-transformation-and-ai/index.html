Digital Transformation and AI

5 courses helping education professionals lead digital transformation in their classrooms and institutions.

Why This Category Matters

Technology is reshaping education faster than at any point in history. In 2024 to 2025, 92% of EU workers reported needing digital skills for their jobs and 30% were already using AI systems in their work, yet most teachers have never received structured professional development on how to integrate these tools effectively and responsibly into their classrooms.

The five courses in this category are built around the most current EU digital competence frameworks, including the newly published DigComp 3.0, and are directly aligned with the Digital Education Action Plan 2021 to 2027. They give education professionals the practical skills, the critical understanding and the professional confidence to lead digital transformation in their schools and institutions.
