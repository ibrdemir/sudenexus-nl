School Leadership and Development

6 courses for education professionals across Europe.

Why This Category Matters

Great schools do not happen by accident. They are built, deliberately and over time, by leaders who understand their institution, inspire their colleagues, manage complexity with clarity and drive continuous improvement with both ambition and humility.

The six courses in this category address the core dimensions of school leadership and institutional development that matter most in European education today. From the daily practice of classroom management to the strategic challenges of school innovation, from Erasmus+ KA1 project management to KA2 cooperation project writing and the full European funding landscape, these courses give education professionals at every level the knowledge, the frameworks and the practical tools they need to lead effectively, sustainably and with genuine impact.
