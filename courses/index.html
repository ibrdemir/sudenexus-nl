Course Catalogue

52 professionally designed courses across three sectors, 15 destinations and all four Erasmus+ 2026 priorities. Everything your institution needs in one place.

Find the Right Course for Your Institution

The Sude Nexus course catalogue brings together 52 Erasmus+ eligible professional development programmes across school education, vocational education and training, and adult learning. Every course is one week in duration, delivered in English, and available across our network of 15 European and Mediterranean destinations. Use the filters below to search by sector, topic, Erasmus+ priority, destination or course level. Or browse the full catalogue by category. If you cannot find exactly what you are looking for, our Design Your Mobility service lets us build something together from scratch.
