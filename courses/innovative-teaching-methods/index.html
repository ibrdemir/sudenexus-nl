Innovative Teaching Methods

6 courses for education professionals across Europe.

Why This Category Matters

The best teachers are not those who deliver content most efficiently. They are those who create the conditions in which students genuinely want to learn, think deeply and develop the competences they need for life beyond the classroom. The six courses in this category address the methodological foundations of outstanding teaching: from the psychology of motivation and game-based learning to project-based and design-based approaches, STEM and STEAM education, outdoor learning and personalised instruction.

Every course in this category is built around the principle that methodology matters, that how you teach is as important as what you teach, and that every education professional deserves access to the best evidence on how to make learning happen.
