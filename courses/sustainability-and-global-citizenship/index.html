Sustainability and Global Citizenship

3 courses for education professionals across Europe.

Why This Category Matters

The climate crisis, the erosion of democratic values, the rise of nationalism and the growing disconnection of young people from civic life are among the defining challenges of our time. Education has a critical role to play in responding to all of them.

The three courses in this category address sustainability and global citizenship from three distinct and complementary angles: the practical, school-based approach to environmental education through Eco-Schools, the broader development of European and global civic identity through Global Citizenship education, and the rich potential of cultural heritage as a resource for intercultural understanding and European identity.
