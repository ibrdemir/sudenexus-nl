Your Professional Identity Is the Starting Point

Every education professional has a different role, a different context and different development priorities. Browsing a catalogue of 52 courses can feel overwhelming. This page makes it simple. Select the profile that best describes your professional role and we will show you the courses most relevant to your work, your learners and your Erasmus+ goals.

You may find that more than one profile fits your situation. That is completely normal. Browse as many as you like. And if you cannot find exactly what you are looking for, our team is always happy to make a personal recommendation.
