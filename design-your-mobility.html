Design Your Mobility

Your institution is unique. Your Erasmus+ programme should be too.

Start the Conversation

Not Everything Fits in a Catalogue

A course catalogue is a starting point, not a limit. At Sude Nexus we know that every school, VET institution and adult education organisation has its own goals, its own culture, its own participants and its own Erasmus+ story. Sometimes a ready-made course is exactly what you need. And sometimes what you need is something that does not exist yet.

That is where Design Your Mobility comes in.

With over 11 years of experience in Erasmus+ project management and professional development across school education, VET and adult learning, we have the knowledge, the network and the creativity to design mobility programmes, training courses, job shadowing placements and learner exchange projects that fit your institution precisely.

Tell us what you need. We will build it with you.

What We Can Create Together

Bespoke Training Courses

A topic not in our catalogue, a combination of topics, a course designed specifically for your staff profile. We design professional development training from the ground up.

Dedicated Group Mobility Weeks

A full mobility week designed exclusively for your institution's staff. Your topic, your destination, your dates, your group. We handle everything from programme design to logistics and documentation.

Job Shadowing Programmes

We arrange job shadowing placements for education professionals who want to observe and learn from a partner institution in one of our 13 destinations or beyond.

Learner Internships and Exchanges

For VET institutions and adult education organisations arranging learner mobility. We support partner matching, placement coordination, participant preparation and all Erasmus+ administrative requirements.

Multi-Destination Mobility Projects

Planning a mobility project that takes different groups to different destinations across the year? We coordinate multi-destination projects from a single point of contact.

Combined Training and Cultural Immersion

A programme that combines structured professional development with an intensive cultural experience in one of our most distinctive destinations.

This Service Is For You If

1

You have specific goals that our standard catalogue does not fully address.

Your Erasmus+ plan has particular priorities, your staff have specific development needs, or your national agency has recommended a focus area that requires a tailored approach.

2

You want your whole staff group to experience something together.

Rather than sending individual teachers to open courses, you want a dedicated mobility week for your own team, in a destination you choose, with content designed specifically for your school culture and development plan.

3

You are working in VET or adult education and need specialist support.

VET learner internships, adult education staff exchanges and job shadowing placements have specific requirements. We know these sectors deeply and design for them with the same quality as our school education programmes.

How the Design Process Works

1

Tell Us What You Need

Use the form below or contact us directly to tell us about your institution, your Erasmus+ project, your participants and what you are trying to achieve. There is no obligation at this stage. We are simply listening.

2

We Listen and Explore

A member of our team will get back to you within one working day. We take time to understand before we recommend. This is where the real design work begins.

3

We Develop a Proposal

Based on our conversation we develop a clear, detailed and costed proposal. You review it, we refine it together until it is exactly right.

4

We Deliver and Support

Once confirmed we handle everything: programme delivery, local coordination, participant support, cultural programme and complete documentation for your Erasmus+ reporting.
